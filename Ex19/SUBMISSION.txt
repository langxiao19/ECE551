========================================
GATED CLOCK DESIGN
========================================
Total Cell Area:        7938.000000
Total Dynamic Power:    1.0359 mW

Design uses:
- Enable-low latches to capture enable signals
- AND gates for clock gating
- Two gated clocks:
  * clk_prod_gated for 32-bit prod_reg
  * clk_accum_gated for 64-bit accum

========================================
COMPARISON
========================================
Area Reduction:     429 units (5.1% improvement)
Power Reduction:    0.0439 mW (4.1% improvement)

Both designs meet timing requirements.

========================================
CONCLUSION
========================================
Clock gating provides measurable area and power savings by:
1. Eliminating 2:1 recirculation muxes (replaced with simpler AND gates)
2. Preventing clock tree toggling when registers are disabled
3. Reducing switching power in 96 total flip-flops when en=0

The 4-5% savings demonstrate the effectiveness of clock gating
for registers with low enable duty cycles.
