TEST 1: OVR_I INSIDE BLANKING WINDOW
========================================
Objective: Verify that 40+ OVR_I pulses occurring INSIDE the blanking 
          window do NOT cause shutdown.

Test Method:
- Applied OVR_I pulses during the blanking period (first ~20 clock cycles)
- Repeated for 45 consecutive PWM cycles
- Each PWM cycle = 2048 clock cycles

Results:
- 45 PWM cycles completed
- OVR_I_cnt remained at 0 throughout entire test
- OVR_I_shtdwn remained deasserted (0)

Conclusion: ✓ TEST 1 PASSED
The blanking window correctly prevented OVR_I events from being counted.

========================================
TEST 2: OVR_I OUTSIDE BLANKING WINDOW
========================================
Objective: Verify that 40+ OVR_I pulses occurring OUTSIDE the blanking
          window DO cause shutdown.

Test Method:
- Applied OVR_I pulses at clock 200 (well outside 128-clock blanking period)
- Repeated for consecutive PWM cycles until shutdown occurred
- Monitored OVR_I_cnt incrementing each cycle

Results:
- PWM Cycles 1-14: OVR_I_cnt incremented from 1 to 14
- PWM Cycle 15: Counter held at 14 (decrement occurred due to 16-cycle rule)
- PWM Cycles 16-32: OVR_I_cnt incremented from 15 to 30
- PWM Cycle 33: OVR_I_cnt reached 31, OVR_I_shtdwn asserted

Conclusion: ✓ TEST 2 PASSED
The overcurrent protection correctly triggered shutdown when OVR_I_cnt 
reached 31 (0x1F) from events outside the blanking window.
